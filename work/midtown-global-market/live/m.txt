Hoyo Sambusa Food Production
Hoyo a Social Benefit Corporation and local producer of Somali sambusa and sauces is a commercial food production facility in the lower level of the Midtown Global Market.
Hoyo produces sambusa, a traditional Somali savory food with a flaky crust and filling- either Beef or Lentil and sauces (Tamarind &amp; Date or Basbaas). Hoyo products (sambusa and sauces) are found at over 62 grocery/coop stores throughout the state. Over 30 Minnesota School Districts serve Hoyo Sambusa to over 300,000 students for school lunches.
