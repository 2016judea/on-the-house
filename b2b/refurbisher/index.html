For T&R Electric · Surplus and sales · Sep 26, 2026

A Missouri city spent $500,000 on transformers from the one supplier it could find on time.

In June, Independence Power & Light bought pole-mount and pad-mount transformers sole-source, because it could not find a second American supplier who would meet its schedule. Your site says T&R supplies “municipalities, REA's, investor-owned utilities” and competes on “inventory, quick delivery.” A municipal utility that could not find quick delivery is exactly who you sell to.

So we went looking for the others. Below: two federal buyers with quotes due this week, three more whose contracts come next, and four Minnesota plants that filed to close this year — each with the record it came from.

How this was done

Federal notices: SAM.gov's public opportunity files, the active file and the FY2026 archive, read Sep 26, 2026, searched for transformer supply, repair and rebuild work. T&R's own words: the Internet Archive's copy of t-r.com from December 3, 2023, since the live site turns away scripts. Council items: our index of public-agency sole-source purchases, built from agenda packets. Closures: Minnesota DEED's WARN notices; where the list names no site, the address is the one printed on the notice. Nothing here is paid data.
