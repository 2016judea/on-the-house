7 of your SBA borrowers lost good standing with the state while they owed Falcon.

We took every Twin Cities loan the SBA's public file lists under Falcon National Bank and still open — 67 loans to 46 borrowers — and looked each borrower up on the Minnesota Secretary of State's register, one at a time.

Every one we found is in good standing today. But 7 were administratively dissolved or terminated at some point after Falcon approved their loan, for missing the annual renewal. Yeej Transport Inc was out for 511 days. Falls Coffee L.L.C. lapsed 16 days after its approval.

5 of them are due to renew again by December 31 this year. A borrower who missed it once is the one worth a reminder in December.

4 names we could not find on the register

The SBA file spells the borrower the way the loan was booked. These spellings match no Minnesota entity exactly. Most will be a spelling or an out-of-state entity. One is worth knowing about: the only SJS Enterprises, LLC on the register is now SJS Rentals LLC, which was terminated from 2013 until June 2025 and is registered on Washburn Ave N, not Penn Ave — so it may not be your borrower at all.

How this was done

Loans: SBA 7(a) and 504 FOIA release, as of Jun 30, 2026, cut to the seven metro counties; “open” means SBA lists it as outstanding or approved-not-yet-disbursed. The SBA publishes the borrower's address, not the project site. Standing: the Secretary of State's public business search, read Sep 26, 2026; a lapse counts only if it started on or after the borrower's first Falcon approval. Nothing here came from Falcon or from any borrower.
