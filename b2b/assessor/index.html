How this was done

Parcels, values, classes, build years and owners: Hennepin's own parcel file, pulled August 14, 2026. Sales: the county's sale of record (retrieved August 18, 2026) with its own arm's-length and usable-comp flags; deeds covering several parcels, or sharing a price and month with another, are dropped. “Value on file” is the market value on that August file, so a sale after January 2 is compared with a value set before it. Businesses: Overture Maps Foundation places, release 2026-08-19, kept only where the listing's street number matches the parcel's. Public and church owners are left out. Nothing here came from your office beyond what it publishes.

This one is free, on us. If there's other work you'd like done, just let us know.
