Our stylists

Justin — owner

During his time at Eric Fisher Salon, Justin really enjoyed going back to the Academy for demos to spread his knowledge to up and coming students. After working for Eric for almost 8 years and doing some work with a hair tool company, Justin decided to buy his own salon in Wichita Kansas, J. Gilman Salon. With a love of being able to educate others, Justin has now also launched his own hair product line, Kasilo.

Daisy

Daisy grew up in Wichita, Kansas, with a lifelong passion for hair styling. She honed her skills and creativity at the Eric Fisher Academy, where she graduated in April 2024. Daisy excels in a variety of services, with a particular love for vivid colors, classic all-over color applications, and intricate braiding. Her artistic background deeply influences her work, allowing her to bring a unique and creative touch to each client. Daisy is dedicated to providing exceptional results and is excited to welcome you to her chair for a personalized and enjoyable experience.
