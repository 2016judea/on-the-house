When digging through city permit records (for a different project) I found something called a 'demolition permit'.

These are issued by the city and signal that a building is being knocked down. Demolished. Which means scrap metal. Copper wiring. Used electrical and HVAC equipment.

And there are guys who make a living doing "junk arbitrage".

If that's you, every one in Minneapolis is on a map. Free —> https://demolition-notice.vercel.app
