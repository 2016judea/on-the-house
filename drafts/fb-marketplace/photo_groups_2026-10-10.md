# Wedding / photo Facebook groups: what each one is (2026-10-10)

For Aidan: 35mm wedding photography plus Super 8, $750 Craigslist post, work at aidanjude.vercel.app/photography-intake.
I read these in headless Chrome on a copy of his FB session. I read only: nothing was posted, commented, reacted to or messaged.
For each public group I read the About page, the rules and the 34–36 newest posts (sorted chronologically). Raw text is in `photo_groups_raw/`.

**One gap:** FB did not show per-post comment counts in the text I could read, so engagement below is judged from the reply threads that rendered inline. It is not a count.

**Join requests sent (all three are pending admin approval):**
- MN Wedding Sell & Swap: no questions asked.
- Twin Cities Wedding Vendor Resources: no questions asked.
- Minnesota MINI Session Finder: answered "MN Photographer" and agreed to the rules.

None of the three can be read until an admin approves.

## Ranked top 3

1. **Minneapolis Photographers.** The only group whose feed carries photographer-to-photographer gigs. On 10/9 Emily Santiago posted *"Looking for a replacement second shooter tomorrow 10/10 — with film experience"* (Wayzata/Delano). Self-promo is banned, but ISO posts are open to answer and sharing photos is the point of the group.
2. **Minnesota Weddings: Find Your Dream Vendors.** Vendor promo is allowed in practice. It holds the closest comparable on record: Maggie Mendenhall, *"I also shoot Super 8 film and 35mm film"*. Couples post asks ("need hair/DJ June 26 2027"). Prices run from $600 (8 hours, Snaps By Corey) to $2,000+ (Elizabeth Daake).
3. **Minnesota Micro Weddings.** These are elopement and backyard couples, the right shape for a $750 film shooter. "No Promotions" is written in the rules, but the feed is almost all vendor promos and none were removed, so the rule is not enforced.

## Everything wedding related Minnesota
public · 14.7K members · 35 new posts today · 1,458 in the last month · created 2015
- **Who posts:** resellers and individuals clearing out decor, plus vendors: DJs, rentals, florals, hair and makeup, gift makers.
- **What the posts are:** about 75% Marketplace-style listings (vases $30, lanterns $100, disco balls $75, dresses $70), plus off-topic sales (a 2009 Santa Fe, earbuds, baseball cards). About 25% are vendor ads (Bee's Knees DJ "$800 today", Complete Weddings, bridal hair 2027). I saw no couples asking for vendors.
- **Vendor promo:** no written rules are shown. Vendor ads are posted freely.
- **Photography prices:** none mentioned in the window.
- **Film or analog:** none.
- **Engagement:** low. Listings get "Message" clicks, not discussion.
- **Admins:** Ashly Soto (Coldwell Banker), Erin Parker (Realtor, Pemberton RE).
- **Verdict:** a wedding garage sale with vendor ads floating on top. A photo post here competes with candle holders and will be scrolled past. Low priority.

## MN Wedding Sell & Swap
private · 25.3K members · 2,573 posts in the last month · created 2015 · **join pending**
- **About and rules:** "for selling all the wedding decorations and other wedding items that you have no use for anymore." Rule 1: *no non-wedding posts or items; spam is removed and your access suspended.*
- **Who posts:** couples and past brides reselling. The feed is unread until the request is approved.
- **Admins:** Mitch and Drew. Full names are hidden to non-members.
- **Verdict:** a buy and sell group for objects. Services are not its purpose and it threatens suspension, so it is not a place to promote. At most it is a place to watch who is getting married (people buying decor for 2027 dates).

## Minneapolis Photographers
public · 8.8K members · 10 new posts today · 288 in the last month · +144 members a week · created 2012
- **Who posts:** photographers (hobbyists and working pros), plus clients and models looking for photographers.
- **What the posts are:**
  - Peer shares: Minnehaha Falls, a drone shot of Grain Belt, Split Rock sunrise.
  - Client asks: "need a photographer for a 17-person family shoot 10/16 in Fridley", "family session Oct 17/18", "ISO end-of-life dog photographer", senior and engagement location questions.
  - Gig calls: a second shooter with film experience for 10/10; We The Peeples media day 10/11; a TFP model collab.
  - Mini-session promos ($200 and $225) and one wedding package ad ("starting at $2,000", Elizabeth Daake).
- **Vendor promo:** rule 3 says *"No Promotions or Spam. Give more than you take… Adding your website or social media to your posts is okay as long as it doesn't become obtrusive."* Photos go in one album per day and should be mostly Minneapolis. Promos still appear and stay up.
- **Photography prices:** $200 and $225 minis; weddings "starting at $2,000".
- **Film or analog:** yes. *"replacement second shooter … with film experience"* (Emily Santiago, 10/10, Wayzata/Delano).
- **Engagement:** the highest of the four public groups. Location questions draw several answers and reply threads; photo shares draw a few.
- **Admins (12):** Michael Rey, Sergey Varyvonchyk, Saibal Kumar Ghosh, Kamal J. Hassan, Cody Allen Coppess, Sarah Kat Fiedler, David Rubene, Luke Luther, Mike Evans, Natalie Champa Jennings, and 2 more.
- **Verdict:** the local photographer community. It is the room where film gigs and second-shooting surface, and where clients ask openly. Aidan can show up by posting his own Minneapolis film frames (one album a day, his site link allowed), and by answering ISO and second-shooter asks.

## Minnesota Weddings: Find Your Dream Vendors
public · 3.0K members · 8 new posts today · 364 in the last month · created April 2025
- **Who posts:** about 80% vendors (DJs, content creators, photographers, venues, MUAs, coordinators). About 10% couples asking. The admin posts "welcome new members" roll calls.
- **What the posts are:**
  - Vendor ads: Bee's Knees $800 DJ, Snaps By Corey "8-hour package $600, 48 hours only", Elizabeth Daake from $2,000, Peridot & Lace 50% off 2028, "$1000 OFF by Oct 15".
  - Couple asks: Valentina (Kabetogama, 6/26/27, hair, makeup and DJ, "hard to find someone who would come up north"); Kristina (hair and DJ, 6/26/27); Hannah Joy (Mora, 10/2/27, florals, catering, hair).
- **Vendor promo:** no written rules are shown. The group description pitches vendor matching, and vendors post freely.
- **Photography prices:** $600 (8 hours) up to $2,000+.
- **Film or analog:** yes. Maggie Mendenhall (Iowa, forestfilm.org): *"I also shoot Super 8 film and 35mm film … keeping the year small (15 weddings total)."* David Esquire: "#Leica loaded" for a Spring Grove wedding.
- **Engagement:** low. Most vendor ads show no inline replies, and couple asks get a few vendor comments.
- **Admin:** Bill Lage ("Digital creator", 4.2K followers).
- **Verdict:** a vendor billboard that couples browse, at a $600–$2,000 price band. Aidan can post one intro post with frames and the intake link, and answer couple asks. A film shooter already posts here with the same pitch, so it is a proven room.

## Minnesota Micro Weddings
public · 1.1K members · 1 new post today · 100 in the last month · +26 members a week · created 2021
- **Who posts:** almost entirely vendors (venues, MUAs, mobile bars, DJs, content creators, photographers). Couple posts were rare in this window.
- **What the posts are:** vendor promos, including:
  - Pla-Mor Ballroom and Stone Lion Winery
  - "Wedding Content Creation $600 / 6 hrs"
  - Emily Roering's 4–6 hour budget photo plus coordination package
  - Delilah Carbajal: *"Anyone doing a micro backyard wedding? … heavily discounted"* (portfolio-building)
  - Corbyn and Morgan Jensen (Sioux Falls)
  - Emily Rivers 2027
  - wedding fairs (Chaska, Anoka 11/7)
- **Vendor promo:** **banned on paper.** The rules say "No Promotions or Spam", "No Sales Posts" and "Micro Weddings and Elopements only". The feed shows these rules are not enforced.
- **Photography prices:** $600 (content creator, 6 hours); "heavily discounted" portfolio offers. Other dollar figures in the feed are venue prices.
- **Film or analog:** none.
- **Engagement:** low. There are almost no inline replies.
- **Admin:** Erin Olsen.
- **Verdict:** an elopement-sized audience, which suits Aidan's offer, but vendors in an unenforced no-promo group are mostly talking to each other. Post only framed as micro or elopement work, and expect few eyes.

## Twin Cities Wedding Vendor Resources
private · 1.3K members · 93 posts in the last month · no new members in the last week · created 2016 · **join pending**
- **About:** "for brides to find vendors as they plan their wedding day. Venue / Reception halls, photographer, makeup/hair stylist, florist, bridal shops etc." It uses FB's Buy-and-Sell format. No written rules are shown to non-members.
- **Is it the planner channel?** No. Despite the name, the About page says it is a bride-facing vendor directory, not vendor-to-vendor or planner-to-vendor.
- **Admin:** Katie. Full name is hidden to non-members.
- **Verdict:** a small, stagnant bride-to-vendor board. It is worth one intro post once approved, but it is not the planner channel the distribution-channel skill favours.

## Minnesota MINI Session Finder | Client & Photographer Connect
private · 524 members · 55 posts in the last month · created March 2026 · **join pending (questions answered)**
- **Rules:** mini sessions only. Photographers may promote minis, themed events and limited-time offers; *"full session promotions, general advertising … not allowed."* Each post must state the date, location, theme, price, what's included and a booking link. One repost per 7 days. Client ISO posts are welcome and photographers may comment on them. **No DMs to clients unless the client invites it.**
- **Admin:** Amy. Full name is hidden to non-members.
- **Verdict:** a mini-session marketplace. Weddings cannot be promoted here. It only fits if Aidan runs a dated, priced film mini (for example, fall film portraits), posted with every required field.
- **Related:** a larger group of the same kind exists, "Minnesota MINI SESSION – Photog & Clients Connection" (private, 5.5K members). There is also "Minnesota Weddings Connect" (private, 18K members). I did not join either.
